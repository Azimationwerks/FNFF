Processing equipment

The lines behind a cut-to-order service.

Washing, precision cutting, preparation and packing, supported by hand-processing teams working to documented specifications.

Processing lines

Four stages, every order

Washing and preparation

Leaf, salad and cut vegetable lines are washed and prepared in a temperature-controlled environment before packing.

Precision cutting

Cutting equipment produces uniform dice, julienne, baton and slice specifications, alongside hand-processing teams for specialty cuts.

Packing

Prepared produce is packed to portion or bulk specification, labelled and moved directly into cool storage.

Despatch

Orders are picked from the cool rooms into cooled loading docks and onto the refrigerated fleet.
