Corporate structure

How the business is organised.

Six operating divisions covering culinary development, processing, quality, logistics, sales and accounts.

Current organisation chart. A named chart showing individual role holders is available to buyers and auditors on request.

Board & executive leadershipFirst Nation Fresh Foods Pty Ltd

312 Victoria Road, Malaga WA 6090 · Strategic and operational governance

Division 01Culinary & Product Development

Professional chefs and cooks developing products and menus to individual customer requirements, with technical support for buyers.

Division 02Hand & Machine Processing

Daily cut-to-order fruit and vegetable processing, washing and preparation to documented cut specifications.

Division 03Quality Assurance & Food Safety

Food safety programs, hygiene practices, hazard analysis and compliance documentation for audit and tender.

Division 04Cool-Chain Warehouse & Fleet

Twelve cool rooms, freezer rooms and blast chillers, cooled loading docks and the refrigerated delivery fleet.

Division 05Sales & Customer Supply

Government and CUA, healthcare, hospitality and remote-site account management and order coordination.

Division 06Accounts & Grower Relations

Grower agreements under the Horticulture Code of Conduct, supplier settlement and customer invoicing.
