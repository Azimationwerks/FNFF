Our mission

What we commit to, and how we deliver it.

Our mission statement, as adopted by the business.

At First Nation Fresh Foods, we are committed to building a strong, proudly Indigenous business that delivers fresh, reliable and innovative produce solutions to local, regional, remote and interstate customers.

We will listen to our customers, work closely with local growers and suppliers, and develop practical solutions that meet the changing needs of the fresh food, hospitality, catering and food service industries.

Our mission is to create meaningful opportunities for First Nations people, support Australian agriculture and local industry, and build a trusted Indigenous-owned brand that delivers lasting value to our customers, communities and future generations.

How we deliver it

We will achieve this by focusing on:

01Sourcing quality produce and products from local Australian growers, manufacturers and suppliers.
02Developing efficient, economical and high-quality fresh and value-added products.
03Creating tailored products and services to meet individual customer requirements.
04Supporting Indigenous employment, training, leadership and business development.
05Building strong and respectful partnerships with First Nations communities, growers and businesses.
06Developing our own proudly Indigenous product range and brand.
07Supporting customers in metropolitan, regional and remote locations.
08Providing dependable supply, responsive service and competitive pricing.
09Reducing food waste, packaging waste and unnecessary environmental impact.
10Exploring environmentally responsible packaging and distribution solutions.
11Reducing transport distances, logistics costs and carbon emissions through local sourcing.
12Maintaining high standards of food safety, quality, workplace health and safety.
13Planning for supply interruptions, emergencies and other force majeure events.
14Operating with honesty, respect, accountability and care for Country and community.

Through strong relationships, local knowledge and shared opportunity, First Nation Fresh Foods will create a sustainable business that connects quality Australian produce with customers while delivering positive outcomes for Indigenous people and communities.
