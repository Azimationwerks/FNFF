Our commitment

Indigenous ownership that reaches further than a certificate.

Genuine Aboriginal ownership, with the commitments to employment, training, partnership and community that go with it.

What it means in practice

Four commitments we hold ourselves to

Ownership100%

Aboriginal-owned

First Nation Fresh Foods is genuinely Aboriginal-owned and led. Buyers working with us support an Indigenous enterprise directly.

PeopleEmployment

Training and leadership

Supporting Indigenous employment, training, leadership and business development as the business grows.

PartnershipCommunity

Communities and growers

Building strong and respectful partnerships with First Nations communities, growers and businesses.

Our own product range

Procurement value

Supporting Indigenous procurement commitments

Government agencies and corporate buyers increasingly carry Aboriginal procurement commitments. Working with First Nation Fresh Foods lets those commitments be met through a supplier with the certification, cool-chain infrastructure and delivery capability that institutional supply demands.

Buyers do not have to choose between social value and operational reliability.
